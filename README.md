
# BIOGRAPHY OF LS BONNEY JOSHUA

## Personal Information

My name is **LS Bonney Joshua**, a 33-year-old man from Winneba in the Central Region of Ghana. I was born on Monday, 15th March 1993.

I began my basic education at **Methodist Junior High School (JHS), Winneba**, where I studied from 2006 to 2009.
